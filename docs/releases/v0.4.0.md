Trips you are on stay in the app, and you can see the weather and the chance of making your connection.

- A bus or shuttle that has left stays on the Commute tab until it arrives. It appears under "Under way now". Tap it, then tap "I'm on this bus" to follow the rest of your journey.
- Weather for the next 6 hours at the stop where you change. Dots show your departures, and the driest hour is marked. Use it to decide if staying later at work is worth it.
- Each journey shows the weather for the hour you wait at the change.
- Bus journeys show the chance that you make the change, for example "92% chance you make the change".
- The steps show when 80% of buses really reach each stop, for example "80% of buses come 17:42–17:51". This uses real GPS times. There is little data so far, so the ranges will improve.
- Weather forecasts are from Open-Meteo.
