Commute now gets your location from your phone automatically.

- The position picker and stop confirmation buttons are removed.
- Android requests location permission when necessary.
- The app updates your location every 20 seconds while Commute is visible.
- Nearby stops and your chosen journey determine the remaining route.
- The app estimates the remaining walk from your phone position.
- Your chosen bus remains tracked after its departure from the transfer stop.
- If location is unavailable, the app shows a status message and uses the last known journey position.

Walking times from the phone position are estimates. Location data stays on your phone.
