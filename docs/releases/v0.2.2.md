The Commute tab now calculates routes from the latest available bus times and your shuttle timetable.

- Select Calculate routes to refresh the times and connections.
- Each leg shows its time source: a stop update, an estimate, or the timetable.
- The planner selects the connection with the earliest arrival.
- If a bus delay changes the connection, the route shows the next available service.
- Each route shows the total journey time, walk time, wait time, and time at each stop.
- Both bus legs use the same feed request. Cancelled buses and stops without service are excluded.
- If live bus times are unavailable, a warning identifies routes that use timetable times.

Routes refresh every minute. Shuttle times come from your saved timetable.
