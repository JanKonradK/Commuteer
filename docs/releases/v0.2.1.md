Bus and train times now show more information about their source.

- Train times use Irish Rail stop updates when available. Model estimates have a separate label.
- Bus times have labels for stop updates, estimates from earlier stops, and timetable times.
- Open Time details to see the source stop, feed age, and timetable times.
- Commute connections show a warning when they use an estimated bus time.
- Delay calculations include time recovered at earlier stops.
- Cancelled buses and stops without service have clear labels.
- The app rejects old bus feeds and refreshes when you return to the app.

An estimate from an earlier stop can differ from Bus Éireann RTPI. It assumes the bus keeps the same delay.
