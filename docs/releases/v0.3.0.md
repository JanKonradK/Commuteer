A redesigned app that is simpler to use and lighter on the battery.

- Three tabs in a floating bar at the bottom: Commute, Timetables and Settings.
- Trains, buses and shuttles are in Timetables. A switch at the top selects the type.
- Settings contains your commute, your shuttle timetables, location information and app updates.
- Refresh is a button beside From and To. The time of the last update is shown below them.
- Long stop names wrap onto more lines and are no longer cut short.
- Content is no longer hidden under the camera or status bar.
- Dark theme with black system bars.
- Tap a journey to see its steps, then tap Follow this journey. A banner shows the journey you are following, with a Stop button.
- All times use the 24-hour clock.

Battery:

- Location is checked every 2 minutes while Commute is open. It is checked every 20 seconds only while you follow a journey.
- Times refresh every minute when a departure is within 30 minutes, and every 3 minutes at other times.
- The app does no work in the background. It updates when you open it again.
