Live bus tracking, a live notification while you travel, and a much quieter screen.

- See where your bus is: "At Moate · 3 stops away · 20 s ago". When you are on board, it counts the stops to yours.
- Open a trip to see the bus on a map. Tap the corner button for a full-screen map you can move around.
- Follow a journey and Commuteer keeps a live notification with a countdown, even with the app closed. It checks live bus times every 30 seconds near departure.
- Alerts: time to leave (5 minutes before), a tight or missed connection, a cancelled bus, and "your stop is next".
- Live bus times are now at most about 30 seconds old, down from about 2 minutes.
- New look, in the style of Memoria with an orchid accent, with far less text: start and destination sit either side of the swap button, no page titles or section headings, icons instead of words for buses, shuttles and walking, and colour only where it means something.
- A green dot marks live times. A crossed-out time is the timetable time it replaced.
- A trip you are already on stays at the top with a green bar, showing when it left.
- The countdown turns amber when you need to leave within 10 minutes.
- In the weather strip, a bus icon marks the hours you wait at the change, and the best hour is highlighted when waiting later is clearly better.
- Location and update times only appear when something is wrong.
- Maps use OpenStreetMap.
