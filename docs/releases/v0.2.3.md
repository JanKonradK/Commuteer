The Commute tab now uses your position and your chosen journey.

- Select where you are: the start, on the first leg, the transfer point, or the next stop.
- Select Choose this journey to save your route on this phone.
- After you board, select On my first leg. The planner retains that service after its departure and checks the next connection with live bus times.
- You can select a recent shuttle departure or change its expected arrival time.
- At the transfer point or bus stop, the app calculates the remaining route without another shuttle journey.
- Use my location suggests nearby stops. Confirm your stop before the planner changes your position.
- Location access is optional. Saved place locations stay on your phone.
- Overnight connections and long waits have clear warnings.

Bus times remain forecasts. A late bus can recover time, so a small connection margin needs care.
